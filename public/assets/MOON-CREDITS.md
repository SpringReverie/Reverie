# Lunar textures

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

Source: CGI Moon Kit, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

- `moon-color.jpg`: web JPEG conversion of `lroc_color_poles_2k.tif` (2019 color map).
- `moon-height.jpg`: `ldem_3_8bit.jpg` (LOLA elevation map).
- `moon-fallback.png`: a spherical projection of the color map with static directional lighting, for browsers without WebGL.

Original color and elevation data: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera and laser altimeter instrument teams. Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA). Scientist: Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).

These visuals are for presentation, not scientific measurement.
